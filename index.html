Our Story

Oscar & Jack's
Brotherhood of the Blade

OJ's Barber Shop isn't just a place to get a haircut — it's a destination built on family, craft, and an unwavering commitment to quality. Founded and operated by brothers Oscar and Jack, every detail in the shop reflects their shared passion for the trade.

Step inside and you'll find a clean, modern environment where the atmosphere is just as sharp as the scissors. Whether you're a longtime regular or walking through the doors for the first time, you'll feel the difference immediately.

Consistency, care, and community — that's the OJ's promise.
